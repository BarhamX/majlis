# Majlis Business Model

## Business Model Canvas

### Customer Segments

- Arab youth.
- Families.
- Arab diaspora.
- Schools and universities.
- Cultural organizations.
- Culture-curious users.

### Value Propositions

- Daily Arab cultural challenge.
- Warm digital majlis experience.
- Short, trusted cultural learning.
- Family and friend competition.
- Shareable cultural pride.
- Safe cultural discussion.

### Channels

- App stores.
- WhatsApp sharing.
- Instagram/TikTok/Snapchat cards.
- University cultural clubs.
- Cultural organizations.
- Influencers focused on Arab identity, language, and heritage.

### Customer Relationships

- Daily app ritual.
- Community discussions.
- Family/friend leagues.
- Premium archive and packs.
- Institutional support later.

### Revenue Streams

- Premium subscription.
- Paid cultural packs/courses.
- School/university licenses.
- Sponsored themed weeks.
- Cultural organization partnerships.

### Key Activities

- Product development.
- Daily content production.
- Cultural review.
- Community moderation.
- Growth and partnerships.
- Analytics and retention optimization.

### Key Resources

- Engineering team.
- Cultural editors/reviewers.
- Content library.
- Brand/design system.
- Community management.
- Partner network.

### Key Partners

- Cultural organizations.
- Museums.
- Universities.
- Heritage festivals.
- Arab creators.
- Schools.

### Cost Structure

- Engineering.
- Hosting and database.
- Content production.
- Moderation.
- Design and creative.
- Growth campaigns.
- Support.

## Revenue Expansion Path

1. Free daily game.
2. Premium archive and regional packs.
3. Private family Majlis.
4. Sponsored cultural campaigns.
5. Institutional licenses.
6. Marketplace for curated cultural learning packs.
