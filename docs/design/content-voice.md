# Majlis Content Voice

## Voice

Majlis speaks like a knowledgeable host: warm, sharp, respectful, and brief.

## Tone

- Playful, not childish.
- Competitive, not humiliating.
- Proud, not preachy.
- Curious, not academic.
- Authentic, not museum-like.

## Copy Rules

- Keep challenge copy short.
- Use questions as hooks.
- Explain meaning in one or two short lines.
- Avoid long historical lectures.
- Avoid regional superiority.
- Avoid claiming certainty when content is disputed.

## Example Hooks

- "This one is easier if you grew up hearing family proverbs."
- "Can you guess what this saying really means?"
- "Your grandfather might know this one. Do you?"
- "Today, the Majlis is testing hospitality."
- "Ask your family before you answer."

## Example Discussion Prompts

- "Which proverb did your family use the most?"
- "What tradition do you think younger people are forgetting?"
- "What is one saying you heard as a child but only understood later?"
- "Which cultural habit should never disappear?"

## Content Length Limits

- Challenge question: 120 characters preferred.
- Answer option: 60 characters preferred.
- Explanation: 250 characters preferred.
- Story of the day: 20-30 seconds when read aloud.
- Discussion prompt: one question only.
