# Majlis User Personas

## Persona 1: The Competitive Arab Youth

**Name:** Omar  
**Age:** 19  
**Context:** University student, active on social apps, enjoys quick daily games.  
**Motivation:** Wants to prove he knows Arab sayings and family traditions.  
**Pain:** Finds cultural learning boring when it feels like school.  
**Majlis Need:** Short challenge, opt-in global leaderboard, and a result that is easy to share with friends.

## Persona 2: The Family Connector

**Name:** Mariam  
**Age:** 34  
**Context:** Parent, wants her children to stay connected to culture.  
**Motivation:** Wants family discussion without forcing lectures.  
**Pain:** Children ignore long cultural content.  
**Majlis Need:** Daily prompt, simple explanation, safe public discussion, and easy external family sharing. Private Family Majlis groups are a post-V1 need.

## Persona 3: The Diaspora Learner

**Name:** Leen  
**Age:** 24  
**Context:** Arab living abroad. Understands culture but feels disconnected from everyday sayings and references.  
**Motivation:** Wants a daily connection to identity.  
**Pain:** Cultural content online is scattered, inconsistent, and not contextualized.  
**Majlis Need:** Short authentic explanations, transliteration later, archive access, family sharing.

## Persona 4: The Cultural Educator

**Name:** Dr. Hassan  
**Age:** 45  
**Context:** University instructor or cultural club advisor.  
**Motivation:** Wants modern discussion prompts for students.  
**Pain:** Existing resources are long and not mobile-native.  
**Majlis Need:** Classroom packs, discussion questions, safe group mode, reporting.

## Persona 5: The Cultural Organization Partner

**Name:** Aisha  
**Age:** 39  
**Context:** Works at a museum, heritage center, festival, or cultural ministry.  
**Motivation:** Wants public engagement around cultural campaigns.  
**Pain:** Campaign content often gets one-time attention and then disappears.  
**Majlis Need:** Sponsored theme weeks, accurate content review, measurable engagement.
