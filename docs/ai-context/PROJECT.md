# AI Context: Project

## Name

Majlis

## Product

A complete, production-running Android Flutter mobile game with a .NET backend.

## One-Line Description

Majlis is a daily Arab culture challenge game that recreates the feeling of a traditional majlis through short puzzles, proverbs, stories, discussion prompts, streaks, and friendly social competition.

## Product Pillars

1. Daily ritual.
2. Arab cultural knowledge.
3. Warm social interaction.
4. Friendly competition.
5. Cultural authenticity.
6. Safe moderated community.
7. Shareable moments.

## Delivery Target

The target is the full Majlis Production V1, not a prototype, reduced release, or isolated feature demonstration. Completion requires an installable Android app and an operational backend with:

- Registration, login, profile, and region preferences.
- Backend-managed daily cultural content.
- Answer submission, scoring, explanation, XP, and streaks.
- Spoiler-safe sharing and deep-link handling.
- Leaderboards and friendly comparison.
- Daily discussion, reactions, reporting, and moderation.
- Admin content creation, review, scheduling, publishing, and moderation tools.
- PostgreSQL persistence, migrations, health checks, logging, analytics, and production configuration.
- Automated core-flow tests and a verified end-to-end Android release flow.

## Delivery Rule

Playable Daily Majlis and Community Majlis are implementation milestones within the full app. Completing one milestone does not complete the product. The release is complete only when `docs/product/full-app-scope.md` and `specs/003-production-app/` are satisfied.

## Post-V1 Product Expansion

- iOS and web clients.
- Full courses and institutional dashboards.
- AI-assisted content generation with editorial review.
- Paid subscriptions and content packs.
- Advanced audio storytelling.
- Broad public social-network behavior beyond the moderated Majlis experience.
