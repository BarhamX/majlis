# Majlis Content Guide

This guide defines how cultural content should be structured, reviewed, and used inside Majlis.

## Content Role in the Product

Majlis is not a generic content library. Content exists to serve the daily game loop:

```text
challenge -> reveal -> short learning -> discussion -> share
```

Every content item should be short, trustworthy, culturally respectful, and usable in a mobile daily experience.

## Length Rules

Mobile-first content should stay short.

Recommended limits:

```text
Title: 3-8 words
Challenge question: 1-2 sentences
Option text: 3-12 words
Explanation: 30-80 words
Story: 50-120 words
Discussion question: 1 sentence
Share hook: 1 sentence
```

## Tone Rules

Content should feel like a smart person in a majlis sharing something interesting.

Use:

- Warm challenge
- Short context
- Clear meaning
- Respectful regional references
- Curiosity hooks

Avoid:

- Lecturing
- Mocking users
- Regional superiority
- Sectarian framing
- Unsupported claims
- Long academic writing

## Cultural Authenticity Rules

Every non-trivial cultural item should eventually include:

- Source or reviewer
- Region/dialect tag when applicable
- Content category
- Review status
- Notes on disputed meanings if relevant

Recommended review states:

```text
Draft
Needs Review
Approved
Published
Archived
Rejected
```

## Example Daily Majlis Item

```text
Title: The Guest Before the House
Topic: Hospitality
Region: Pan-Arab
Difficulty: Easy

Challenge:
What does "الضيف ضيف الله" most closely mean?

Options:
A. A guest must bring gifts
B. A guest is honored as a trust
C. A guest should not stay long
D. A guest should cook with the host

Correct Answer:
B

Short Explanation:
The saying reflects the value of honoring guests. In many Arab households, hosting is treated as a duty, a source of pride, and a sign of generosity.

Proverb:
الضيف ضيف الله

Discussion Question:
What is one hospitality habit your family still keeps?

Share Hook:
Can you solve today's Majlis proverb?
```

## Content Safety Checklist

Before publishing content:

- [ ] Does it avoid insulting any region, tribe, sect, or nationality?
- [ ] Is the meaning explained clearly?
- [ ] Is it short enough for mobile use?
- [ ] Is it tagged correctly?
- [ ] Does it avoid fake certainty for disputed content?
- [ ] Is the challenge answer unambiguous?
- [ ] Does the discussion prompt invite respectful conversation?

## AI/LLM Content Rule

AI may help draft content, but culturally meaningful content should not be published solely because an AI generated it.

Before publishing AI-assisted content:

- Review by a human editor.
- Verify meaning and source where possible.
- Check regional sensitivity.
- Rewrite to match Majlis tone.
